Rider Essentials
Getting around is incredibly simple. Just log into your verified campus account, select your preferred way to travel from the main layout board, and set your pickup spot. You will be connected with a neighborhood driver in moments.
Scootz™ is our custom micro-mobility option designed for quick, nimble trips across campus walkways. If you are rushing between classrooms or heading to a nearby dining hall, Scootz™ gives you instant, flexible access to local electric scooters right from the palm of your hand.
Campus Trust & Safety
Safety is our absolute foundation. We maintain a community of drivers consisting entirely of background-checked, campus-vetted students and university staff. Every driver goes through rigorous identity validation to ensure complete peace of mind for every trip.
To keep the network exclusive and trusted, everyone must sign up using their official university email address. We verify your academic status instantly before granting access, ensuring that anonymous or unverified individuals can never enter the app.
The Local Boundary
We operate within an intentional 5-mile boundary radiating from the center of campus. This deliberate setup turns the app into a premium neighborhood utility, covering all student housing, local grocery spots, popular off-campus restaurants, and student hangouts while keeping the network localized and hyper-focused.
