Our Mission

Insulated Campus Mobility Matrix

The Standard of Trusted Safety. TrypCar, Inc. was born out of a simple reality: moving between institutional facilities should be effortless, pristine, and entirely safe. We deliberately replaced the unpredictable public rideshare lottery with an exclusive, closed peer-to-peer network infrastructure.

Every compact EV sedan and TrypScoot in our fleet is piloted or operated strictly by identity-verified members of your immediate community. By verifying students, parents, faculty, and institutional staff through a secure corporate authentication gateway, we ensure total peace of mind and complete asset accountability.
