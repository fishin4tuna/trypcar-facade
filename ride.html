How it will work

From request to drop-off in four simple steps.

  1. Sign in

    Open the TrypCar app and sign in with your verified university email.

  2. Set your pickup

    Choose your current location or the nearest TrypCar Hub, then pick where you're headed.

  3. Track your ride

    See your vehicle and driver before they arrive, follow them on the map, and share your trip with a friend.

  4. Arrive

    Hop out at your destination. Payment happens in the app, so there's nothing to fumble with.

Built different from rideshare

Every part of TrypCar Ride is planned around one company standing behind the whole trip.

Company-owned vehicles

No personal cars. Every ride will be in a TrypCar vehicle, maintained and inspected on a regular schedule.

Screened, trained drivers

TrypCar drivers will pass background checks, motor vehicle record reviews, and safety training before their first ride.

Members only

Riders verify through their university email, so everyone in the network is part of your campus community.

Live tracking and trip sharing

Follow your ride in real time and send your trip to a friend or family member with one tap.

Simple campus pricing

Fares are planned to be clear and upfront inside your campus zone, designed with student budgets in mind.

5-mile campus zone

Rides will cover campus and the nearby places your campus community goes most, like off-campus housing and grocery stores.

Questions about Ride

When can I start riding?

TrypCar Ride will be available once TrypCar launches on your campus. Reserve your account now and we'll email you as soon as your campus goes live.

How much will rides cost?

Pricing will be announced before launch. Our goal is clear, upfront campus fares designed with student budgets in mind.

Can I ride off campus?

Yes, within your campus service zone. TrypCar plans to cover a 5-mile zone around each campus, including nearby housing and shopping.
