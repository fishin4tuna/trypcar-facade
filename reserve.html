How it will work

Set it once and stop worrying about getting there.

  1. Pick a time

    Choose your pickup time, up to 14 days ahead.

  2. Make it repeat

    Turn one ride into a weekly schedule that matches your classes or workday.

  3. Get a reminder

    The app reminds you before pickup, so you're ready when your ride arrives.

  4. Ride

    Your TrypCar vehicle and driver meet you at the time and place you set.

Made for campus schedules

Reserve is planned around the rhythm of campus life, for students, faculty, and staff alike.

Book up to 14 days ahead

Lock in rides for the week ahead or the week after.

Recurring rides

Set up repeating rides for classes, shifts, or standing meetings, and adjust them anytime.

Pickup reminders

Notifications before every scheduled ride, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Easy changes

Edit or cancel a scheduled ride right in the app when plans change.

Big-day peace of mind

Book your ride the night before an exam, presentation, or important meeting, and focus on what matters.

Same fleet, same standard

Every scheduled ride will use TrypCar-owned vehicles and professionally screened drivers.

Questions about Reserve

How far ahead can I book?

TrypCar plans to let members schedule rides up to 14 days in advance.

Can I set up rides for my whole semester?

Recurring rides are planned, so you can set a weekly schedule that matches your classes or work schedule and change it as needed.

What if my plans change?

You'll be able to edit or cancel scheduled rides in the app. Full scheduling and cancellation policies will be announced before launch.
