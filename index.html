Why our own fleet

Most rideshare apps rely on drivers' personal cars. TrypCar will own, maintain, and stand behind every vehicle in its network.

One standard for every vehicle

Every ride will happen in a TrypCar-owned vehicle, maintained and inspected on a regular schedule.

Screened, trained drivers

TrypCar drivers will pass background checks and safety training before their first campus ride.

One company accountable

The car, the driver, and the ride all answer to one company, with campus safety built in from day one.
