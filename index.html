The Standard of Trusted Safety

TrypCar was born out of a simple reality: moving between institutional facilities should be effortless, pristine, and entirely safe. We replaced the unpredictable public rideshare lottery with an exclusive, closed peer-to-peer network.

Every compact EV sedan and TrypScoot in our fleet is piloted or operated strictly by identity-verified members of your immediate community, ensuring total peace of mind for students, faculty, and parents alike.
