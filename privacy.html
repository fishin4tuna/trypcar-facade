Privacy Policy

How TrypCar, Inc. collects, uses, and protects your information during our pre-launch stage.

Last updated: November 21, 2026
Status: Pre-launch. TrypCar is in development and not yet operating.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TrypCar, Inc. ("TrypCar," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit trypcar.com or submit information through its forms (together, the "Website"). This policy is part of our Terms of Service.

TrypCar is not yet operating. We do not currently offer an app, rides, scooter rentals, payments, or member accounts. Before we launch those services, we will publish an updated Privacy Policy that covers them.

2. Who the Website Is For

Our forms are intended only for people who are at least eighteen (18) years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under eighteen. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you believe someone under eighteen has submitted information to us, please email info@trypcar.com.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

We collect only the information you choose to submit through our forms:

  • Member portal (account reservation): your email address, the role you select (such as student, parent, faculty or staff, or investor), and your agreement to our Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy.
  • Contact form: your name, email address, the topic you select, and your message.
  • Emails you send us: anything you include when you write to info@trypcar.com.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, the services that host and deliver our Website may automatically receive basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and the date and time of your visit. TrypCar does not use advertising trackers or analytics tools on the Website.

3.3 Cookies and Browser Storage

The Website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. It uses your browser's temporary session storage only to remember that you have already seen our opening animation during your current visit. This information stays on your device and is cleared when you close your browser.

4. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • respond to your questions and messages;
  • keep track of account reservations and send launch updates and related news about TrypCar;
  • understand interest in TrypCar, such as which roles and campuses are signing up;
  • protect the Website against spam, fraud, and misuse; and
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

5. How We Share Information

TrypCar, Inc. does not sell your personal information and does not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We share information only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run the Website and our email, which may use your information only to provide their services to us. These currently include Web3Forms (delivers form submissions to us), Zoho Mail (our email provider), GitHub Pages (hosts the Website), and Google Fonts (delivers the Website's lettering).
  • Legal requirements: when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of any person, TrypCar, or the public.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of our business, subject to the commitments in this Privacy Policy.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep reservation and contact information for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and to keep you informed about TrypCar's launch, or until you ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it longer to comply with the law or resolve a dispute.

7. How We Protect Information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for the Website and its forms and limited access to the inbox where submissions are received. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Stop updates: email info@trypcar.com with the subject "Unsubscribe," and we will stop sending you launch updates.
  • Access, correction, or deletion: email us to ask what information we have about you, to correct it, or to delete it. We will respond within a reasonable time and as required by applicable law.
  • Additional rights: depending on where you live, you may have other privacy rights under state law. Contact us and we will honor requests as applicable law requires.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including before TrypCar launches its services. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. If we make significant changes, we will let reservation holders know by email.

10. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Email TrypCar, Inc. at info@trypcar.com.
