Privacy Policy

TrypCar, Inc. Privacy Policy Protocol
Last Updated: October 29, 2026
Classification: Public Compliance Documentation • Corporate Data Safeguard

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TrypCar, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("TrypCar," "we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit trypcar.com, use any TrypCar mobile application, take a TrypCar ride, rent a Scootz scooter, or otherwise use our services (together, the "Services"). It also explains the choices and rights you have regarding your information.

This Privacy Policy is part of, and is governed by, the TrypCar Terms of Service. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Terms of Service. By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

2. Who the Services Are For

The Services are offered only to members of the Campus Community who are at least eighteen (18) years old. TrypCar does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under eighteen. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a person under eighteen, we will delete it and close the associated Account. If you believe a person under eighteen has provided us with information, please contact us through the contact page at trypcar.com.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

We collect the information you give us directly, including:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, mobile phone number, password, and profile photo;
  • verification information, such as your institutional email address, student or employee identification card, enrollment or employment status, and, for Scootz rentals where required by law, driver's license information;
  • payment information, such as your card or other payment details, which are collected and stored by our third-party payment processors rather than by TrypCar, except for limited details such as the card type and last four digits;
  • communications, such as messages you send to TrypCar support, safety reports, ratings, reviews, and survey responses; and
  • any other information you choose to provide, such as saved locations or emergency contacts.

3.2 Information Collected Through Your Use of the Services

When you use the Services, we automatically collect:

  • location information, including precise location from your device when the TrypCar app is open or when you are on an active Ride or Scootz rental, and approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • trip and rental information, such as pickup and drop-off locations, routes, dates, times, distance, duration, fares, and the vehicle or scooter used;
  • device and usage information, such as your device type, operating system, browser, IP address, app version, crash reports, pages viewed, features used, and the dates and times of your activity;
  • Scootz telemetry, such as scooter location, speed, battery level, and where and how a rental began and ended; and
  • safety recordings, where TrypCar Vehicles are equipped with in-vehicle cameras or recording devices. Any such recording will be disclosed by notice in the vehicle and is used as described in Section 4.

3.3 Information From Others

We may receive information about you from your college or university to confirm your eligibility, from payment processors about the status of transactions, from background-check and identity-verification providers where permitted by law, from Drivers and other riders who report incidents or submit ratings, and from law enforcement or insurers in connection with accidents or investigations.

3.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, secure the Platform, prevent fraud, and understand how the Services are used. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but some features of the Services may not work properly without them.

4. How We Use Information

We use personal information to:

  • create and manage your Account and verify your eligibility;
  • provide the Services, including arranging Rides, dispatching TrypCar Vehicles, unlocking and tracking Scootz Scooters, calculating fares, and processing payments;
  • promote safety, including screening, monitoring trips and rentals, responding to emergencies, investigating accidents, complaints, and safety incidents, and enforcing our Terms of Service;
  • detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, misuse, security incidents, and unlawful activity, including violations of the acceptable-use rules in our Terms of Service;
  • provide customer support and communicate with you about your Account, your trips, and changes to the Services;
  • send you marketing communications, where permitted by law and subject to your choices;
  • maintain, analyze, and improve the Services, including service areas, routing, fleet management, and new features;
  • comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests from authorities, and meet insurance and regulatory requirements; and
  • establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

5. How We Share Information

TrypCar does not sell your personal information, and does not share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes. We share personal information only as described below:

  • with Drivers, limited to what is needed to complete your Ride, such as your first name, profile photo, and pickup and drop-off locations;
  • with service providers that perform services on our behalf, such as payment processing, cloud hosting, mapping, identity verification, customer support, communications, and analytics, who are permitted to use your information only to provide those services to TrypCar;
  • with your college or university, limited to what is needed to confirm your eligibility or to address a safety incident involving the campus;
  • with insurers, claims adjusters, and legal advisers in connection with accidents, claims, and disputes;
  • with law enforcement, government authorities, or other parties when we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law or legal process, or is necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of any person, TrypCar, or the public;
  • with your emergency contacts or emergency services, if you use an in-app safety feature or if we believe there is an emergency involving your safety;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of TrypCar's business, subject to the commitments in this Privacy Policy; and
  • with your consent or at your direction.

We may also share information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it can no longer reasonably identify you.

6. How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information for as long as your Account is active and as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to resolve disputes, investigate safety incidents, prevent fraud, meet insurance requirements, and comply with legal, tax, and accounting obligations. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it. Safety recordings are kept only for a limited period unless they are needed to investigate an incident, respond to a claim, or comply with the law.

7. How We Protect Information

TrypCar uses administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encryption of information in transit, access controls that limit who can view personal information, and monitoring for unauthorized access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information. You can help by using a strong, unique password and keeping your login credentials confidential. If we learn of a security breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

8. Your Choices

8.1 Account Information

You can review and update your Account information at any time in your Account settings.
