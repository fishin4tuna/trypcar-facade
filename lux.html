How it will work

Premium rides, as easy to book as any other.

  1. Choose Lux

    Select Lux when you request or schedule a ride in the app.

  2. Bring your group

    Add how many people are riding, and TrypCar will match a vehicle with room for everyone.

  3. Ride now or book ahead

    Grab a Lux ride on the spot, or schedule it ahead for a big event.

  4. Arrive together

    Everyone shows up at the same time, in the same car, in style.

The premium tier of TrypCar

Lux is planned for the moments when the ride is part of the night.

Upscale fleet vehicles

Late-model, larger vehicles from the TrypCar fleet, kept to the same maintenance standard.

Room for your group

One vehicle for the whole group instead of splitting up into several rides.

Bookable for big events

Schedule ahead for formals, game days, and celebrations through TrypCar Reserve.

Same screened drivers

Lux uses the same professionally screened, trained TrypCar drivers as every other ride.

Split the fare

Fare splitting is planned, so groups can share the cost right in the app.

Members-only safety

Every rider is a verified member of the campus community, even on the busiest nights.

Questions about Lux

How many people can ride in a Lux vehicle?

Lux is planned around larger vehicles built for groups. Exact vehicle options and seating will be announced before launch.

Can I book Lux for a specific event?

Yes. You'll be able to schedule Lux rides ahead of time through TrypCar Reserve.

How much will Lux cost?

Lux will be priced as a premium option. Pricing will be announced before launch, and fare splitting is planned for groups.
