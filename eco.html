How it will work

Choose a cleaner ride, and share it if you want to save more.

  1. Choose Eco

    Select Eco to ride in an electric or hybrid TrypCar vehicle.

  2. Opt into sharing

    Choose a shared ride to be matched with other members heading the same way.

  3. Ride together

    Share the trip along the same campus route and split the fare.

  4. See your impact

    Track the emissions you've saved over the semester right in the app.

Better for your wallet and your campus

Eco is planned to make the greener choice the easy choice.

Electric and hybrid vehicles

Eco rides will use the electric and hybrid vehicles in the TrypCar fleet.

Lower fares when you share

Shared rides are planned to cost less than riding solo, since everyone splits the trip.

Matched along your route

Shared trips will pair members heading the same direction, to keep detours short.

Share with verified members only

You'll only ever share a ride with verified members of your campus community.

Track your savings

See the emissions you've helped avoid across the semester.

Fewer cars on campus

More people per trip means less traffic and less congestion around campus.

Questions about Eco

Do I have to share my ride?

No. You'll be able to choose a solo electric or hybrid ride, or opt into sharing to save more.

Who will I share a ride with?

Only other verified TrypCar members from your campus community who are heading in the same direction.

How much will I save by sharing?

Shared rides are planned to cost less than solo rides. Exact pricing will be announced before launch.
