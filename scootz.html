How it will work

From the app to the next building in four steps.

  1. Find a Scootz

    See nearby scooters and their battery levels in the TrypCar app.

  2. Scan to unlock

    Scan the scooter's code with your phone and you're ready to go.

  3. Ride

    Glide across campus at campus-friendly speeds.

  4. Dock at a Hub

    End your ride at a TrypCar Hub dock, ready for the next member.

Questions about Scootz

Do I need a separate account for Scootz?

No. Scootz is planned to work with your regular TrypCar account, in the same app you use for rides.

Where can I ride a Scootz?

On designated campus routes and areas, following campus and local rules. Details will be set for each campus before launch.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Like all TrypCar services, Scootz will be available only to members 18 and older. Wearing a helmet is always encouraged.
