How it will work

A simple meeting point for every ride.

  1. Find a Hub

    The app shows the nearest TrypCar Hub when you request a ride.

  2. Walk over

    Hubs will sit along everyday campus paths, close to where students already are.

  3. Meet your ride

    Your TrypCar vehicle pulls up at a clearly marked spot. No hunting for the right curb.

  4. Or grab a Scootz

    Hubs will double as docks for Scootz electric scooters for shorter trips.

Why Hubs matter

Smart pickup points make campus rides faster, calmer, and easier to find.

Well-lit and visible

Hubs will be placed in bright, high-visibility areas, which matters most on late nights.

No curbside confusion

A known meeting point means no guessing which side of the building your ride is on.

Faster during class changes

Concentrating pickups at Hubs is planned to keep things moving during the busiest times of day.

Home base for Scootz

Every Hub is planned to include Scootz docks, linking cars and scooters in one network.

Planned around campus life

Hub locations will be chosen around dorms, libraries, dining halls, and campus entrances.

Easy to reach

Hubs are intended to sit along accessible paths that every member can get to comfortably.

Questions about Hubs

Do I have to use a Hub?

No. You'll also be able to request pickup at your current location inside your campus zone. Hubs are simply the easiest, most visible option.

Where will Hubs be located?

Hub locations will be set for each campus before launch, near the places students go most, such as dorms, libraries, and campus gates.

Can I park a Scootz at any Hub?

That's the plan. Hubs are designed to serve as docking points for Scootz electric scooters.
