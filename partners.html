Questions from businesses

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Founding Partner spots are free during pre-launch. Paid promotion options, such as in-car and in-app placements, will open at launch, with preferred pricing for Founding Partners.

What kinds of businesses can join?

Restaurants, coffee shops, retailers, salons, gyms, entertainment venues, housing, and service providers near campus. We review each application to keep the network a good fit for members.

When will my listing appear?

Founding Partner listings will be published on this page as partners are confirmed ahead of launch.

Am I committing to anything?

No. Joining is a no-cost expression of interest. Any paid promotion at launch would be a separate agreement that you choose to make.
